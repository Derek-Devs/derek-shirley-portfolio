<CaseSection number="01" kicker="Problem" title="Marketing data was spread across five major platforms">

Analysts spent more than 35% of their time preparing data by hand, and funnel and cross-channel ROI reporting was hard.

Leadership also needed a Q4 media budget recommendation on a three-hour deadline. Web traffic was down about 42% year over year.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection number="02" kicker="System" title="Marketing data in one Databricks environment" rail="5+ sources unified">

I designed a Databricks environment that brought more than five marketing data sources together. I also improved Adobe Analytics reporting and set up UTM and attribution governance.

<CaseSection number="03" kicker="Method" title="The budget model and the web traffic analysis">

I built a media-contribution model to test the outside firm’s Q4 proposal of about $11M.

For the web traffic decline, I found that click-through rates fell 40% to 75%, depending on the comparison, which pointed to weaker messaging. That drop was a major driver of the decline. I presented the findings to VP leadership.

Separately, I designed and prototyped the company’s first internal marketing mix model (MMM) in Google Meridian. The MMM stayed a prototype. The Q4 recommendation came from the separate media-contribution model.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection number="04" kicker="Constraints" title="A three-hour deadline">

The Databricks environment was still being built when leadership needed the Q4 recommendation. I had three hours to build a model for the CEO and CMO, so I used the data available then.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection number="05" kicker="Decision" title="The $6.9M recommendation">

The CEO and CMO used the $6.9M recommendation in their planning. The Databricks environment became the shared source for marketing KPIs, executive reporting and self-service analysis in Power BI.

<CaseSection number="06" kicker="Impact" title="Less manual reporting and new ROI analysis" rail="≥35% less manual reporting">

Manual reporting fell by at least 35%. With more than five sources in one place, cross-channel ROI analysis became possible.

The Q4 model recommended $6.9M against an outside proposal of about $11M. Falling click-through rates were an important driver of the 42% traffic decline.

</CaseSection>
