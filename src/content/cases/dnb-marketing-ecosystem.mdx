<CaseSection number="01" kicker="Problem" title="Marketing data was spread across separate platforms">

Marketing data was spread across five major platforms. Analysts spent more than 35% of their time manually preparing data, and reporting on the marketing funnel and cross-channel ROI was difficult.

Leaderboard and Summer Season Pass lacked consistent performance reporting. Leadership also needed a Q4 media budget recommendation on a three-hour deadline, while web traffic was down about 42% year over year.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection number="02" kicker="System" title="Integrating marketing data in Databricks" rail="5+ sources unified">

I designed a Databricks-centered marketing-data environment integrating more than five sources, improved Adobe Analytics reporting, and established UTM and attribution governance.

<CaseSection number="03" kicker="Method" title="Budget modeling and web-traffic analysis">

I built a media-contribution model to evaluate the outside proposal of about $11M for Q4. In the web-traffic analysis, I identified a 40% to 75% deterioration in CTR or message resonance, depending on the comparison, as a major driver of the decline. I presented those findings to VP leadership.

I separately designed and prototyped the company’s first internal Marketing Mix Model using Google Meridian. The MMM was not deployed and was not the model behind the Q4 recommendation.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection number="04" kicker="Constraints" title="A three-hour deadline for the Q4 recommendation">

The Databricks environment was still being built when the Q4 recommendation was needed. I had three hours to prepare a model for the CEO and CMO, so the recommendation used the data available at the time.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection number="05" kicker="Decision" title="The $6.9M Q4 recommendation">

The CEO and CMO used the $6.9M recommendation in their planning. The Databricks environment became the shared source for marketing KPIs, executive reporting, and self-service analysis in Power BI.

<CaseSection number="06" kicker="Impact" title="Reporting and analysis results" rail=">=35% less manual reporting">

Manual reporting fell by at least 35%. Integrating more than five sources made cross-channel ROI analysis possible and gave Leaderboard and Summer Season Pass consistent performance reporting.

The Q4 model recommended $6.9M against an outside proposal of about $11M. The web diagnosis identified weaker CTR and message resonance as an important driver of the 42% traffic decline.

</CaseSection>
