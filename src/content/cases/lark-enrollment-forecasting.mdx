<CaseSection number="01" kicker="Method" title="Forecast inputs and seasonality" rail="Three partner forecasts">

I built the forecasts from data pulls showing the relationship between audience or list size, marketing sends, and resulting enrollments. I accounted for seasonality and projected one year ahead for Carelon, BCBSAL, and MMO.

<CaseSection number="02" kicker="Validation" title="Monthly checks for each partner">

I compare forecast enrollments with actuals each month, separately for Carelon, BCBSAL, and MMO, after accounting for backdated enrollments.

As of September 2026, monthly enrollment error has usually been within 10% for each partner during the year. The underlying monthly series and a baseline comparison are not published here.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection number="03" kicker="Decision" title="Outreach and budget planning">

The forecasts inform how much outreach to send and how to budget direct mail. Email costs are also considered in planning, even though email is often treated as free.

These forecasts project enrollment from observed relationships and do not estimate causal lift. The team also uses the [attribution model](/work/lark-attribution) to review where past enrollments came from.

</CaseSection>
