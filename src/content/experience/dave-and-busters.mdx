I worked on marketing planning, web performance, attribution, and the data environment behind executive reporting.

### Q4 budget recommendation

On a three-hour deadline, I built a media-contribution model and recommended a $6.9M Q4 marketing budget against an outside proposal of about $11M from an external analytics consultancy. The analysis shaped CEO and CMO planning.

### Web-traffic analysis

I led analysis of a 42% year-over-year decline in web traffic. The work identified a 40% to 75% deterioration in CTR or message resonance, depending on the comparison, as an important driver. I presented the findings and implications to VP leadership.

### Data architecture

I designed a Databricks-centered marketing-data environment integrating more than five sources, improved Adobe Analytics reporting, established UTM and attribution governance, and reduced manual reporting by at least 35%.

### Marketing mix modeling

I designed and prototyped the company’s first internal Marketing Mix Model using Google Meridian. The MMM was not deployed; the Q4 recommendation above came from a separate media-contribution model.

### Analyst selection and development

I selected and recommended the candidate who was hired into Marketing, then trained him after he joined.
