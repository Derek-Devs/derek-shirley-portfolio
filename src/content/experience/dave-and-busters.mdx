I gave leadership a $6.9M Q4 budget recommendation, found a key driver of falling web traffic and cut manual reporting by at least 35%.

### $6.9M Q4 budget recommendation

On a three-hour deadline, I built a media-contribution model and recommended a $6.9M Q4 marketing budget. An outside analytics consultancy had proposed about $11M. The recommendation shaped CEO and CMO planning.

### Web traffic decline

I led the analysis of a 42% year-over-year drop in web traffic. An important driver was click-through rates that fell 40% to 75%, depending on the comparison, which pointed to weaker messaging. I presented the findings to VP leadership.

### Marketing data in Databricks

I designed a Databricks environment that brought more than five marketing data sources together. I also improved Adobe Analytics reporting and set up UTM and attribution governance. Manual reporting fell by at least 35%.

### Marketing mix model prototype

I designed and prototyped the company’s first internal marketing mix model (MMM) in Google Meridian. The MMM stayed a prototype. The Q4 recommendation came from the separate media-contribution model.

### Hiring and training an analyst

I selected and recommended the candidate Marketing hired, then trained him after he joined.
