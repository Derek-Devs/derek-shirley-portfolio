### Commercial pricing

I priced competitive freight bids for customers including Nestlé and BODYARMOR using market rates, lane history, prior coverage, carrier capacity, serviceability, margin, customer requirements, and changing market conditions. Pricing had to win business while remaining profitable and operationally deliverable.

### Pricing workflow and measurable adoption

I designed a HubSpot field pricing-request workflow and data contract, built video and hands-on training, and drove adoption. Response time improved about 43% and win rate improved about 22%. The work contributed to a period of about 26% annual company revenue growth.

### Analytics leadership

I helped establish the company’s first analytics function and data stack, directly managed and developed one analyst, and trained another team lead.
