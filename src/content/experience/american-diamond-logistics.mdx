I priced freight bids and established the company’s pricing analytics department during the post-COVID freight market.

### Commercial pricing

I priced competitive freight bids for customers including Nestlé and BODYARMOR using market rates, lane history, prior coverage, carrier capacity, serviceability, margin, customer requirements, and changing market conditions. Pricing had to win business while remaining profitable and operationally deliverable.

### Pricing requests and training

I designed a HubSpot workflow for pricing requests, defined the required data, and trained colleagues through videos and hands-on sessions. Response time improved about 43% and win rate improved about 22%. The work contributed to a period of about 26% annual company revenue growth.

### Analytics leadership

I established the pricing analytics department and its data stack. I selected and trained a colleague from another team to join the department, where I directly managed one analyst.

I also provided continuous coaching for logistics coordinators and senior logistics coordinators, alongside video and hands-on training for the pricing workflow.
