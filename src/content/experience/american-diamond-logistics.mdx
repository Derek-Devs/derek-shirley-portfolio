I built the company’s pricing analytics department and priced freight bids in the post-COVID freight market.

### Freight bid pricing

I priced competitive freight bids for customers including Nestlé and BODYARMOR, using market rates, lane history, carrier capacity and margin. Every bid had to win the business, make money and be deliverable.

### Pricing request workflow

I designed a HubSpot workflow for pricing requests, defined the data it needed and trained colleagues with videos and hands-on sessions. Response time improved about 43% and win rate about 22%. The work contributed to a period of about 26% annual revenue growth for the company.

### Building the department

I set up the department’s data stack. I selected a colleague from another team to join, then trained and managed them directly. I also coached logistics coordinators and senior logistics coordinators.
